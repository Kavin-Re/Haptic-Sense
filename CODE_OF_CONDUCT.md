# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text at that link applies; this is a summary.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Haptic-Sense a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, welcoming and constructive.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it kindly.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, trolling, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful.

## Reporting

To report a problem, open an issue at https://github.com/Kavin-Re/Haptic-Sense/issues without including sensitive details, and ask for private follow-up. Maintainers will respond and handle reports with discretion.
